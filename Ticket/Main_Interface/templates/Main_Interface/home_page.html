Instructions to Purchase Tickets

Please review these important details before completing your booking.

Booking and payment

  • Tickets can be purchased online up to 10 days in advance.
  • Pay using bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay, Tap, or debit/credit cards including Mastercard, Visa, and DBBL Nexus. More payment options will be available soon.

Refunds and ticket delivery

  • If a payment fails after money is deducted, your bank or MFS provider should refund it within 8 business days.
  • If money is deducted but you get no ticket confirmation, the refund also takes up to 8 business days.
  • No confirmation email? Check your Spam or Junk folder, or download the ticket from your account’s purchase history after logging in.

Use the official app

  • Download the official Rail Sheba app by Bangladesh Railway from Google Play.
  • Bangladesh Railway is not liable for passengers who download fake apps or other apps on Google Play that claim to sell railway tickets.

Extra coach fare

Tickets from an extra coach carry an additional charge on top of the regular fare.
